# Draft news release — not published

Free Energy Help Ltd has completed a research computing experiment using a fully synthetic six-site business-energy procurement model. FEH calculated an exact classical optimum before comparing it with sampled results from a quantum-computing run.

Across 1,024 measured outcomes, the exact classical optimum ranked second and appeared 228 times, or 22.2656%. The most frequent candidate appeared 247 times, or about 24.12%, and had a slightly worse objective score: 17.04 compared with the exact optimum of 17.00.

The result is a technical feasibility milestone, not evidence of commercial advantage. FEH's next research stage is repeatability testing, statistical stability and comparison with strong classical optimisation baselines.

All procurement data in the reported experiment was synthetic. No live customer data, supplier-confidential pricing or production procurement decisions were used.
