## Economics model

Do not calculate or rank a channel by cost per qualified opportunity unless spend and qualification evidence refer to the same attributable cohort.

For each channel retain:
- attributable spend;
- leads;
- qualified opportunities;
- quotes/tenders;
- won contracts;
- evidenced commission/revenue;
- cost per lead;
- cost per qualified opportunity;
- cost per won contract;
- revenue-to-spend ratio when revenue is evidenced.

Unknown values stay "Not connected", not zero.

## Decision boundary

Scale only from sufficient attributable evidence. Use small controlled tests with predeclared success criteria. Pause when deliverability, quality or attribution is uncertain. Stop on compliance failure or persistently poor economics. No automatic budget movement is authorised.
