# FEH Backlink Engine — legitimate editorial shortlist

Status: INTERNAL / NO OUTREACH / NO SUBMISSION / NO PAYMENT / NO PUBLICATION.

Purpose: shortlist only genuine editorial and regional opportunities where an FEH page would be a relevant reader resource. A backlink is a possible editorial consequence, never the condition of coverage.

## Tier 1

### Energy Live News
- Route: genuine story/editorial submission route is published separately from advertising.
- FEH target page: Quantum Procurement Lab page, only after the six-part commercialisation pack is complete and claim-checked.
- Story angle: small UK commercial-energy consultancy documents a reproducible real-hardware procurement optimisation feasibility experiment, with explicit limits and no claim of quantum advantage.
- Exact next action: prepare a 100–150 word factual pitch and one link to the evidence-controlled Lab page; HOLD, do not send.
- Reject if: the story would need sponsored placement, exaggerated savings, IBM endorsement or a guaranteed link.

### Talking Utilities
- Route: publication explicitly accepts editorial enquiries, press releases and guest-piece pitches for the UK energy/utilities commercial and technology community.
- FEH target page: Quantum Procurement Lab or, later, an evidence-led public methodology page.
- Story angle: what a small commercial-energy intermediary learned from testing optimisation workflow feasibility on real quantum hardware, including reproducibility limits.
- Exact next action: draft a concise practitioner-led guest-piece abstract after the clean pack is reconciled; HOLD, do not send.
- Reject if: requested content becomes advertorial, link-exchange, paid follow-link or unsupported product promotion.

## Tier 2

### Electrical Times
- Route: publication invites article contributions and news items; editorial is separate from advertising.
- FEH target page: corrected MPAN/MPRN guide or a commercial-metering explainer.
- Story angle: practical supply-point identifiers for commercial electricity/gas customers, with a precise distinction between the 13-digit MPAN Core and wider bill presentation.
- Exact next action: finish the MPAN/MPRN corrections first, then prepare a technical explainer outline; HOLD, do not contact.
- Reject if: page remains factually uncorrected or the placement requires link payment.

### East Midlands Chamber
- Route: Chamber adviser has confirmed free editorial consideration for members in Business Network Magazine and member news submissions to the weekly Chamber News e-shot.
- FEH target page: corrected Health Check / renewal guide for business-help content; Quantum Lab for a regional innovation story.
- Commercial dependency: 2026 membership price is employee-band based; no membership decision has been approved.
- Exact next action: keep as a channel-economics decision, not an outreach action. If membership is later approved, submit only genuinely newsworthy material through the member route.
- Reject if: joining is justified only by backlink value or if expected lead economics do not support the fee.

## Tier 3 / conditional

### DataCentral
- Route: genuine editorial contribution page for UK/European data-centre infrastructure, including power and energy.
- FEH target page: only a genuinely data-centre-specific energy/procurement article backed by direct experience/evidence.
- Exact next action: HOLD unless FEH develops a data-centre-specific case or research angle.
- Reason: credible publisher but narrower vertical fit than Tier 1/2.

### Business Peak District
- Route: public news-submission form and regional business-news coverage.
- FEH target page: local/regional FEH company news or practical business-energy guide.
- Exact next action: HOLD; only use when there is a real Derbyshire/Peak District business-news hook.
- Reason: legitimate regional opportunity, but lower subject fit than energy/editorial publications.

## Hard reject policy

Reject:
- PBNs and link farms;
- bulk low-quality directories;
- profile/comment/forum spam;
- hidden, cloaked or injected links;
- irrelevant sites;
- reciprocal-link schemes presented as editorial;
- paid follow-link arrangements;
- "guaranteed DA/DR" packages;
- sponsored content masquerading as earned editorial;
- duplicate press-release syndication whose only objective is link volume;
- any target asking FEH to weaken factual, compliance or disclosure standards.

## Publication gate

No pitch or submission is allowed until the destination page is factually correct, has a stable public URL, has no placeholder telephone data, contains no unsupported prevalence/savings claim, and has an internal claim/evidence record.
