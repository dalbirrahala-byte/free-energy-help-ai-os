## Free Energy Help completes real-hardware quantum procurement feasibility experiment

**Derby, 30 September 2026 — DRAFT FOR REVIEW**

Free Energy Help Ltd has completed an exploratory computing experiment using a fully synthetic six-site commercial-energy procurement model and real IBM Quantum hardware.

The study first established the exact classical optimum for the frozen synthetic problem, then sampled a one-layer QAOA circuit on the `ibm_marrakesh` quantum processing unit.

Across 1,024 measured outcomes, the exact classical optimum appeared 228 times, or 22.2656%, and ranked second by frequency. The most frequently measured candidate appeared 247 times, or about 24.12%, and had a slightly worse objective score: 17.04 compared with the exact optimum of 17.00. The retained job record reported two seconds of QPU usage.

FEH is treating the result as a technical feasibility milestone, not as evidence of quantum advantage or commercial superiority. The next proposed research stage is controlled repeatability testing and comparison with strong classical optimisation baselines before any increase in problem size.

The experiment used synthetic data. No live customer information, confidential supplier pricing or production procurement decisions were involved.
