# SEO/AEO research note — 30 September 2026

Draft only; no publication.

Ofgem sources reviewed tonight support three content priorities: a clearer business-contract-renewal explainer, a separate electricity supply-point identifier explainer, and a separate gas supply-point identifier explainer. The renewal page must distinguish deemed, out-of-contract and rollover/evergreen arrangements and avoid unsupported claims that most contracts automatically renew.

The renewal page should keep the Free Business Energy Health Check as the main internal CTA. The identifier pages should explain the distinction between a supply-point identifier and a physical meter serial number, use privacy-safe examples only, and route visitors needing help to the Health Check.

Authoritative sources:
- https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/your-energy-supply/how-manage-your-energy-supply/get-business-energy-contract
- https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/guidance/change-tenancy-businesses
- https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/finding-your-energy-supplier-or-network-operator
