## Channel economics gate

The existing FEH channel-economics engine already requires a common observation window and OBSERVED_VERIFIED inputs before channels can be compared. It calculates:
- raw-lead cost;
- cost per qualified opportunity;
- qualified-to-signed conversion;
- cost per signed contract.

Budget reallocation and campaign activation remain false even when the dashboard is measurable.

## Minimum common ledger

For each channel, record the same window and:
- spend in integer pence;
- raw leads accepted;
- qualified opportunities;
- signed contracts;
- source/reference proving the figures;
- whether the figures are observed/verified or estimated.

Use cost per qualified opportunity and cost per signed contract for commercial review. Cost per raw lead is diagnostic only.

## Current implication

Do not compare Apollo, live-transfer providers, Google Ads, editorial/referral, Chamber or organic social using headline lead price alone. The next useful comparison starts only when FEH can count qualified opportunities and signed contracts on the same definitions and window.
