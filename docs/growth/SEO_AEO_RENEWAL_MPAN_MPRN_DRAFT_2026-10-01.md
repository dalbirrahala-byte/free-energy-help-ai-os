## 1. Business energy contract renewal

### Recommended H1

Business Energy Contract Renewal: What to Check Before Your Contract Ends

### Direct answer

If a fixed business energy contract is approaching its end date, first check the contract end date, switching window, notice requirements and any termination terms. Ofgem says fixed-rate business contracts include a switching window that opens before the end of the contract. If a fixed contract has already expired, the business may instead be on a rolling/evergreen contract, out-of-contract rates or a deemed-rate contract. These are different arrangements and should not be presented as interchangeable.

### Where do I start? — seven steps

1. Find the current supplier and contract end date.
2. Read the contract terms for the switching window, notice requirements and any exit conditions.
3. Check whether the current arrangement is fixed, rollover/evergreen, out-of-contract or deemed.
4. Gather recent electricity and gas bills and confirm the supply points involved.
5. Check the MPAN for each electricity supply and MPRN for each gas supply.
6. Compare suitable contract options, including the incumbent supplier where appropriate, on more than headline unit rate alone.
7. Record the chosen next action and deadline so the account does not drift without review.

### Contract-status explainer

**Rollover or evergreen contract**  
Ofgem says some contracts automatically renew at the end of a fixed term if no new arrangement has been made or the customer has not switched. Terms and notice requirements depend on the contract.

**Out-of-contract rates**  
A business can move onto out-of-contract rates after a fixed contract expires. Check the supplier's terms and current rates, and compare available options.

**Deemed-rate contract**  
A deemed contract is a contract the customer has not expressly chosen, for example after moving into premises and consuming energy before agreeing a contract. It is not simply another name for every expired contract.

### FAQ draft

**When can I switch a business energy contract?**  
It depends on the contract. Ofgem says fixed-rate contracts normally have a switching window before the end date. Check the contract or ask the supplier for the applicable dates and terms.

**What happens if my fixed business energy contract has expired?**  
You may be on a rolling/evergreen contract, out-of-contract rates or a deemed-rate contract depending on the circumstances and supplier arrangements. Confirm the position before comparing options.

**Are deemed and out-of-contract rates the same thing?**  
No. Ofgem lists deemed-rate contracts and out-of-contract rates separately. The correct label depends on how the supply arrangement arose.

**Should I automatically change supplier at renewal?**  
No. Ofgem notes that the best option may be with the current supplier. Compare the relevant offers and terms before deciding.

**What information is useful for a renewal review?**  
Supplier, contract end date, recent bills, consumption, meter/supply details and MPAN/MPRN where applicable.

## 2. MPAN page

### Recommended H1

What Is an MPAN? How to Find Your Electricity Supply Number

### Direct answer

An MPAN identifies an electricity metering point. Elexon defines the **MPAN Core** as a 13-digit identifier made from the Distribution Identifier, Unique Reference Number and Check Digit. A traditional full Supply Number shown on some electricity bills can display additional technical fields around that 13-digit core, including profile and metering information. FEH should therefore avoid describing the 13-digit MPAN Core itself as “a 21-digit number”.

### Suggested structure

- What an MPAN identifies
- Where to find the MPAN Core on an electricity bill
- Difference between the MPAN Core and the wider Supply Number presentation
- MPAN versus meter serial number
- Why a business may need an MPAN for a supplier, switch, tenancy or meter query
- What to do if the bill is unavailable

### Accuracy lock

Do not say that the first two digits of the lower 13-digit core are the Profile Class. In Elexon's composition, Profile Type is a separate field in the wider MPAN presentation; the 13-digit core is made from items 4, 5 and 6.

## 3. MPRN page

### Recommended H1

What Is an MPRN? How to Find Your Gas Supply Number

### Direct answer

An MPRN (Meter Point Reference Number) is the identifier for a gas supply point. Xoserve describes it as a six- to 10-digit number that appears on gas bills in Britain. It identifies the supply point/site, not the physical meter itself.

### Suggested structure

- What an MPRN identifies
- Where to find it on a gas bill
- MPRN versus gas meter serial number
- When a business may need the MPRN
- What to do if there is no recent gas bill
- Change-of-tenancy and new-premises checks
