## Draft 1 — contract end dates

A business energy contract ending does not always mean the same thing happens next.

Depending on the contract terms and supplier, a business may move onto an out-of-contract rate, a rolling or evergreen arrangement, or in some circumstances a deemed contract.

A practical first step is to confirm the contract end date, notice terms, current supplier, annual usage and MPAN/MPRN before comparing options.

Draft CTA: Free Business Energy Health Check.

Fact-control note: do not use out-of-contract and deemed as synonyms.

## Draft 2 — MPAN and the S-number

The electricity MPAN is the 13-digit core supply number used to identify the electricity supply point.

On many bills it sits inside a larger boxed S-number layout containing additional metering information, which is why more than 13 digits can appear around it.

Draft CTA: FEH MPAN guide / Health Check.

## Draft 3 — MPRN check

An MPRN identifies the gas supply point, not the physical meter.

Xoserve describes an MPRN as a six-to-10-digit number. It normally appears on the gas bill and remains tied to the supply point even if the meter is changed.
