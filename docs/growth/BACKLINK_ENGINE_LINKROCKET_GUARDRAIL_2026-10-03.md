## Trigger

A 3 October email from LinkRocket's founder asked for feedback after FEH had signed up for a free LinkRocket account. The email offered entry into a gift-card drawing for replies.

No reply was sent.

## Current public offer

LinkRocket currently presents itself as an all-in-one SEO platform with:
- backlink analysis;
- site audits;
- keyword/rank tracking;
- AI-visibility monitoring;
- a Backlink Exchange;
- a Publisher Marketplace.

The public pricing page currently shows a free tier and paid plans, including a $59/month Pro tier after trial.

## FEH decision

### Allowed as read-only research

Use the existing free account, without adding payment details, only for:
- backlink inventory / referring-domain observation;
- technical SEO/site-audit findings;
- keyword/rank observation;
- AI-visibility observation;
- competitor-gap research.

Any finding remains advisory until independently checked.

### Rejected / held

Do **not** use:
- reciprocal Backlink Exchange links as a backlink KPI;
- marketplace purchases where placement, anchor text or follow status is purchased;
- bulk link packages;
- PBN, link-farm, profile/comment or hidden-link offers;
- any arrangement requiring FEH to link out merely to obtain a return link.

A publisher listed in a marketplace may only be reconsidered independently if it has a genuine editorial process and FEH can pitch a useful story without purchasing link treatment.

## Evidence standard

Before any target is promoted to READY_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW, record:
- real editorial ownership and contact route;
- topical/regional relevance;
- recent original editorial content;
- no obvious PBN / republished-template footprint;
- no guaranteed follow-link or exact-match-anchor sale;
- the FEH asset that genuinely fits the publication;
- an exact no-payment/no-reciprocity next action.

## Exact next action

Run only the free site/backlink/AI-visibility diagnostics already available to the existing FEH account, then compare any recommended publisher/domain against the FEH editorial acceptance checklist.

Do not reply to the founder, enter the gift-card feedback offer, upgrade, buy a placement or use the exchange overnight.
