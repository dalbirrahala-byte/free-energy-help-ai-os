## Rejected / do-not-use routes

- The Manufacturer single press-release publication route previously identified as requiring payment: do not classify as earned editorial or backlink outreach.
- Any advertorial, sponsored-content or corporate-partnership package where link placement is part of the purchase: commercial advertising may be evaluated separately, but not as an earned-backlink tactic.
- Generic “write for us” farms with no real editorial audience.
- Reciprocal-link exchanges, hidden/cloaked placements, forum/profile/comment drops and mass directory submissions.

## Story angle guardrails

Permitted angle:
- UK SME explores a reproducible hybrid quantum/classical workflow for a synthetic commercial-energy procurement problem.
- Real IBM Quantum hardware was used in a retained synthetic six-site experiment.
- The classical optimum was observed in the hardware sample distribution, with exact counts and ranking reported.
- The next research step is controlled repeatability.

Do not pitch:
- “quantum advantage”, “breakthrough savings”, “cheaper energy through quantum”, “IBM-backed”, “production-ready”, or any claim that FEH has validated customer savings.

- Energy Live News contact page exposes a distinct story-submission route separate from advertising.

- East Midlands Chamber states that editorial coverage is free for members, with no guarantee of publication, and lists October 2026 as its Energy theme.

No publication, contact, form submission, payment or account creation is authorised by this shortlist.
