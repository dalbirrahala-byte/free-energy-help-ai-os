Permitted:
- FEH tested a synthetic six-site commercial-energy procurement optimisation workflow on IBM Quantum hardware.
- The classical optimum appeared in the measured distribution and ranked second by frequency in the retained run.
- The next step is controlled repeatability and classical benchmarking.

Not permitted without new evidence:
- quantum advantage;
- customer savings;
- cheaper supplier prices;
- IBM partnership, endorsement or validation;
- production readiness;
- claims that the hardware mode was the optimum.
