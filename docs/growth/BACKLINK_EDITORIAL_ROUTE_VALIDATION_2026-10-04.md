## Conditional / reject-for-link-building

FEH rule:
- unpaid discretionary editorial consideration may be assessed on normal editorial merit;
- the paid fast-track route must **not** be used as a backlink tactic;

Source:

## Universal rejection rules

Reject:
- PBNs;
- bulk directory packages;
- comment/profile spam;
- reciprocal-link schemes whose primary purpose is ranking manipulation;
- hidden/cloaked links;
- irrelevant sites;
- paid follow-link or anchor-text guarantees;
- guaranteed editorial coverage sold mainly for SEO;
- any publisher asking FEH to disguise sponsored placement as independent editorial.

## Promotion gate

No publisher contact until:
1. target FEH page is accurate and live;
2. claim/evidence register is current;
3. the story has a genuine reader/news value;
4. human approval is recorded;
5. no paid-link condition is attached.

Backlink remains a possible by-product of genuine editorial coverage, never the requested deliverable.
