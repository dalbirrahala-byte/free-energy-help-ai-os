## Draft 1 — Renewal status clarity

Business energy contract ending soon?

Three terms are often confused:
- rolling / evergreen;
- out-of-contract rates;
- deemed-rate contract.

They are not automatically the same thing. Your position depends on the contract, supplier and circumstances.

Start with the facts: contract end date, notice terms, recent bill, annual consumption and the correct electricity/gas supply identifiers.

Free Energy Help can help a business organise those facts before it decides what to do next.

Target page after factual QA:
Business Energy Contract Renewal / Free Business Energy Health Check.

Evidence boundary:
Use current Ofgem contract-status guidance. Do not claim every expired contract becomes deemed and do not quote a universal notice period.

## Draft 2 — Change of tenancy checklist

Moving into or out of business premises?

Energy accounts can become messy when the change of occupier is not recorded promptly.

Useful evidence to keep:
- move-in / move-out date;
- opening or closing meter reads where available;
- supply address;
- current supplier details;
- MPAN for electricity and MPRN for gas;
- documents showing the change of occupier.

Ofgem advises businesses to tell the current supplier about a change of tenancy as soon as possible, whether moving in or moving out.

Target page:
Change-of-tenancy guidance / Free Business Energy Health Check.

Evidence boundary:
Do not promise that FEH can override supplier evidence requirements or instantly reconnect a disconnected supply.

## Draft 3 — MPAN versus MPRN

MPAN and MPRN sound similar, but they identify different supply points.

MPAN:
electricity supply-point identifier. The MPAN core is 13 digits; on some bills it sits inside a wider Supply Number display.

MPRN:
gas supply-point identifier. Xoserve describes it as a six- to 10-digit number shown on gas bills in Britain.

Neither should be confused with the physical meter serial number.

Target pages after preview QA:
MPAN guide / MPRN guide / Business Energy Bill guide.

Evidence boundary:
Do not describe the MPAN core simply as a 21-digit identifier. Use Xoserve's current six-to-10-digit MPRN wording.
